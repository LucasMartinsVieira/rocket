# Library and lists

## Browse and track titles

- Search the **Library** by title, alternate title, or creator. Use the media-type and status filters to narrow the view, and switch between covers and rows as needed.
- Tap a title to see its details. Its status can be **Planned**, **In Progress**, **Completed**, **Paused**, **Dropped**, or **Revisiting**.
- Use **Your note** for one editable note and **Journal** for separate dated entries. Personal ratings and start/end dates are optional.
- Touch and hold a title for quick actions. On a list title, this still opens the title overview rather than starting a drag.

## Create and organize lists

1. Open **Lists** and create a list. Choose its media type, name, icon, and accent. A list can contain only titles of its chosen type.
2. Open the list to add more titles. You can also manage a title's list memberships from its details.
3. To change the order, open **Reorder titles**, hold and drag a title, then tap **Save**. Other titles shift as you drag, and **Cancel** leaves the saved order unchanged. Screen-reader actions can move a title up or down.
4. To change the look, tap **Accent** in the list header, or use the pencil next to the name to change the icon.

Removing a title **from a list** or deleting a list doesn't delete titles, notes, or journals from your library. Deleting a title **from the library** also removes its note, journal, and list memberships.
