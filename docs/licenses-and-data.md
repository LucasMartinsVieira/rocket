# Licenses and data sources

Rocket’s source code is [MIT-licensed](https://github.com/LucasMartinsVieira/rocket/blob/main/LICENSE). Third-party dependencies come with their own license notices. The MIT license does not grant rights to metadata, descriptions, cover art, or other material retrieved from external catalogs. Each provider’s terms and any applicable rights held by the original owners still apply.

| Provider | Terms and content rights |
| --- | --- |
| **OMDb** | OMDb labels its content [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Its [terms](https://www.omdbapi.com/legal.htm) restrict use to personal, noncommercial purposes. Review them before redistributing catalog content, even for free. |
| **IGDB** | [IGDB's API docs](https://api-docs.igdb.com/#getting-started) describe noncommercial use under the Twitch Developer Service Agreement and direct monetized projects to the partnership program. User-supplied Twitch credentials aren't a substitute for a partnership or permission. |
| **Open Library** | The Internet Archive [doesn't assert new copyright](https://openlibrary.org/developers/licensing) over its database material. Existing rights can still apply to individual contributions and cover art. |
| **Tenrai** | [Tenrai's terms](https://tenrai.org/terms) allow personal and commercial projects, including caching, subject to the terms and third-party rights. That permission doesn't license the underlying MyAnimeList content or artwork. |

## Before distributing an APK broadly

Don't bundle catalog dumps, downloaded cover art, or real library backups as sample data. For public screenshots, use artwork you created or have permission to publish. Leave provider and dependency notices intact.
